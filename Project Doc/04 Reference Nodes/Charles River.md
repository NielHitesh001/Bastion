# Charles River

## Role in this vault

Charles River describes its IMS as an investment-management platform with an integrated multi-asset order and execution management system (OEMS), portfolio/risk functions, compliance, and post-trade workflows.

## Mechanics and design considerations

An OEMS differs from a trading strategy engine: it owns the order lifecycle, connectivity, allocations, controls, and audit trail. Verify asset-class, venue, and FIX coverage during implementation.
